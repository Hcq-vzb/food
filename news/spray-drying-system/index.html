Spray Drying System

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machine

The purpose of this guide is to shorten the spray drying system decision for a food ingredient buyers buyer. Rate first, then the specification points, then the acceptance checks that turn a quotation into a working machine.

What This Equipment Does

Because a spray drying system handles a defined step for food ingredient buyers, it can be isolated, measured and improved. This build is specified for 330 ml can product at 1704–2354 units per hour, with changeover handled by configuration rather than by hand.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare rated capacity, contact-part material, control architecture and changeover time rather than headline speed. This reference spray drying system uses 316L stainless steel for product-contact surfaces, a 4-inch HMI, a 24–35 kW connected load and a 1200 × 2800 × 2800 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

The upstream buffer decides how much downtime a spray drying system can absorb. With 1704–2354 units per hour and a 10-minute changeover, the buffer has to cover the change rather than the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity is only worth what you can sell. A spray drying system at 1704–2354 units per hour should be sized against contracted demand for 330 ml can product, otherwise the ROI depends on a market the food ingredient buyers operation does not control.

Maintenance and Service

A spray drying system that is easy to maintain is one your team will actually maintain. On this build the wear items are visible, the intervals are published and the reset procedure fits on one page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the actual connected load of the spray drying system?

The reference build draws 24–35 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

How long does a format changeover take on this spray drying system?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 10 minutes. The first change to a new 330 ml can format takes longer.

What accuracy can this spray drying system hold in real production?

The reference build holds 3.0 ml on 330 ml can product at 1704–2354 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
