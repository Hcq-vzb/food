Frequently Asked Questions

Can the food grade silicon dioxide be upgraded later?

Within the 2321–3321 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 5 L range before ordering.

What spare parts should be held for the food grade silicon dioxide?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 2321 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.
