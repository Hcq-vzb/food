What This Equipment Does

The test of a food grade emulsifying salt is not the demonstration but the third month. At 1841–2491 units per hour for 500 ml can product, this configuration is designed to hold its rate once the novelty of commissioning has worn off.

Key Technical Specifications

Read a food grade emulsifying salt specification for what is measurable: rate 1841–2491 units per hour, deviation ±1.0 ml, container 500 ml can, control 7-inch colour touch, load 1.5–2.5 kW, footprint 1600 × 1900 × 2800 mm, changeover 10 minutes. This build is quoted that way.

Integration With Your Line

The best commissioning plan names three people: one for the 1.0 ml verification, one for the encoder handshake, one for the cleaning route. With a 1841–2491 units per hour machine, that is what a week-long delay costs.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Run the ROI on the worst month, not the average one. A food grade emulsifying salt rated at 1841–2491 units per hour will look different in peak season than in a quiet month, and the investment case has to survive both.

Maintenance and Service

Treat the food grade emulsifying salt service records as production data. Once a few months of interventions are logged, the failure pattern on the seals and the drive usually tells you where the real risk sits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 500 ml can product the standard grade is not appropriate.

Does the food grade emulsifying salt need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1600 × 1900 × 2800 mm GA drawing.

What does the food grade emulsifying salt do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
