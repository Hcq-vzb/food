Frequently Asked Questions

Can the food grade phosphates be upgraded later?

Within the 1826–2476 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 330 ml range before ordering.

Is a 316L stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 330 ml product the standard grade is not appropriate.
