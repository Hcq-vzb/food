Frequently Asked Questions

Can the food grade glycerin be upgraded later?

Within the 5373–6873 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 20 L range before ordering.

Is a food-grade 304 stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 20 L product the standard grade is not appropriate.

Does the food grade glycerin need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1800 × 2800 × 3200 mm GA drawing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
