Frequently Asked Questions

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

How long does a format changeover take on this natural food colorant?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 8 minutes. The first change to a new 1.5 L format takes longer.

How does the natural food colorant behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the natural food colorant determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.
