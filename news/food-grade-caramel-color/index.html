What This Equipment Does

A food grade caramel color should be judged on the range it handles, not the peak it advertises. This configuration covers 330 ml can product at 1401–2051 units per hour, which is the span most food ingredient buyers production lines actually demand.

A food grade caramel color is properly specified when an auditor can check it. Rated capacity 1401–2051 units per hour, contact parts food-grade 304 stainless steel, control 4-inch HMI, load 38–53 kW, footprint 1800 × 1400 × 2400 mm, changeover 20 minutes — this build carries all six.

Integration With Your Line

A food grade caramel color fits an existing food ingredient buyers production line when three things align: the transfer pitch, the control protocol and the cleaning route. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and works across a 1800 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Three mechanisms produce the return on a food grade caramel color: less material lost, fewer hours worked, less time stopped. At a 0.5 ml tolerance and 1401–2051 units per hour, all three move together for a food ingredient buyers operation.

Maintenance and Service

A food grade caramel color that is easy to maintain is one your team will actually maintain. On this build the wear items are visible, the intervals are published and the reset procedure fits on one page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the food grade caramel color do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

Can the food grade caramel color run without a dedicated operator?
