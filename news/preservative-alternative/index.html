Preservative Alternative

Published 2026-10-26 · KIWL Machine

Specifying a preservative alternative for food ingredient buyers is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

Ask a food ingredient buyers plant engineer to describe their best-performing preservative alternative and they will describe the one that runs for weeks without intervention. This configuration is specified at 1265–1915 units per hour on 500 ml product with that as the design target.

Key Technical Specifications

Every quotation for a preservative alternative contains a headline rate and a long list of options. The four entries that actually determine the outcome are the 3.0 ml tolerance, the 304 stainless steel contact-part grade, the 34–49 kW load and the 3200 × 1900 × 2800 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Downstream transfer matters as much as upstream buffer. If the preservative alternative can hand over at 1265–1915 units per hour without accumulation, the rest of the line keeps its own cadence.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare a preservative alternative against the cost of the alternative rather than against zero. The manual step it removes at 1265 units per hour usually outweighs the purchase price inside the first operating year for a food ingredient buyers operation.

Maintenance and Service

The preservative alternative was specified with wear part lead times in mind, so a food ingredient buyers operation is not left waiting a week for a seal while the line stands at 1265 units per hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the preservative alternative suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 1265–1915 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

How is the preservative alternative documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

What spare parts should be held for the preservative alternative?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 1265 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
