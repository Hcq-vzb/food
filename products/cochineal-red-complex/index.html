Cochineal Red Complex

Cochineal red complex is formulated for a specific colour strength and stability, so buyers specify carminic acid content and shade rather than the raw material name.

Key Specifications

E numberE120
Carminic acid2-5 percent
FormLiquid / powder
SolubilityWater soluble
Dosage10-80 ppm
pH range3-8

A preparation rather than a single compound

Cochineal red complex is formulated for a specific colour strength and stability, so buyers specify carminic acid content and shade rather than the raw material name.

Colour shifts with pH

It is red at acid pH and shifts toward purple as pH rises, so the target pH of the product determines the shade achieved and the dose needed.

Typical Applications

  • Dairy desserts and yogurt — 20-60 ppm for a stable pink.
  • Beverages — 10-50 ppm at acid pH for a clear red.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does it differ from carmine?

Both are E120; the complex is a formulated preparation with specified strength and handling properties.

Why did my shade change?

pH shift. It is red below pH 5 and purple above pH 7.

Is it heat stable?

Yes, which is the main advantage over natural alternatives.
