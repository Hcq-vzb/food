Sweet whey with a functional specification

Cremac is a sweet whey powder selected for creaminess and low heat damage, which keeps solubility high and avoids the cooked note that over-heated whey carries. It is used where whey contributes body rather than being a cheap filler.

Where whey earns its place

In bakery it improves crust colour through lactose browning; in ice cream it adds solids without the cost of milk powder; in confectionery it contributes milk notes at lower cost than whole milk powder.

Typical Applications

  • Bakery and biscuits — 2-5 percent improves crust colour and flavour through lactose browning.
  • Ice cream and dairy — 3-6 percent replaces part of the milk solids and adds body.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it the same as whey protein concentrate?

No. This is sweet whey powder at 11-13 percent protein, a bulk dairy solid, not a protein concentrate.

Is it suitable for lactose-intolerant consumers?

No, it is 70-75 percent lactose.

What is the shelf life?

12 months in unopened bags below 25 degrees C and 65 percent humidity.
