CLA (Conjugated Linoleic Acid)

CLA is an oil and is inconvenient in capsule and sachet formats.

Key Specifications

CLA content60-80 percent
FormsOil / spray dried powder
Dosage1.5-3 g per day
SourceSafflower oil
Packaging20 kg

Spray dried powder solves the handling problem

CLA is an oil and is inconvenient in capsule and sachet formats. Spray drying onto a carrier gives a free-flowing powder that disperses in water, at some cost in payload.

Formulation stability

CLA oxidises readily, so an antioxidant system and opaque packaging are part of the product design rather than optional extras.

Typical Applications

  • Sports nutrition — 1.5-3 g per day in capsules or powder blends.
  • Weight management products — As a functional ingredient with the appropriate claims in the target market.

Frequently Asked Questions

Oil or powder?

Oil for capsules at higher payload; powder for sachets and beverage blends.

Is it stable in storage?

Moderately. Use antioxidants and keep sealed, cool and dark.

What claims are permitted?

Varies widely by market; structure-function claims need local legal review.
