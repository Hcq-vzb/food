Cocoa Mass

Packaging25 kg block / 1,000 kg pallet

Alkalisation sets colour and flavour

Natural cocoa mass is acidic and sharp; alkalised (Dutched) mass is darker, milder and more soluble. The degree of alkalisation is the main lever a formulator has over colour and taste in chocolate and bakery.

Fat content drives processing

At 52-58 percent fat, cocoa mass contributes both solids and cocoa butter, which changes the fat balance of a recipe. Formulations specify the fat content rather than the mass quantity for this reason.

Typical Applications

  • Dark chocolate — 30-45 percent cocoa mass plus cocoa butter and sugar.
  • Bakery and ice cream — 2-8 percent for colour and cocoa flavour in batters and frozen desserts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Natural or alkalised?

Natural for fruit-forward and high-acid profiles, alkalised where a darker colour and milder taste are wanted.

What is the difference from cocoa powder?

Mass is the ground nib with full fat; powder is pressed cake with most fat removed.

How should it be stored?

Below 20 degrees C, away from odour, in a dry room. Cocoa butter absorbs foreign odours readily.
